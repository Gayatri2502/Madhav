Capabilities

End-to-end engineering

01

Structural Design

SolidWorks & ArtiosCAD modeling. FEA compression testing. ISTA 1A–3E simulation. Right-sized packaging reduces material 15–30%.

02

Pre-Press & Graphics

Full-color flexo, litho-lamination, and digital print. Pantone matching. Inline varnish/aqueous coating. 10-color capability.

03

Die-Cutting & Finishing

Rotary & flatbed die-cutting. Kiss-cut, perf-score, window patching. Gluing: crash-lock, auto-bottom, 4/6-corner. Inline folding.

04

Quality Assurance

Mullen burst, ECT, flat crush, pin adhesion, Cobb water absorption. Climate-controlled conditioning (TAPPI T402). Full traceability.

05

Just-in-Time Delivery

Vendor-managed inventory. Kanban replenishment. Blanket orders with scheduled releases. 24hr emergency run capability.

06

Sustainability

100% recyclable. 30–100% post-consumer fiber. FSC® certified. Lightweighting programs. Carbon footprint reporting.
